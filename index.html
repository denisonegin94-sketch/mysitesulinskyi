How I work

01 — Business Goals

I start with your business goals, not with AI tools. Understanding what drives revenue and where friction lives.

02 — Functions, not roles

Moving away from traditional 'roles'. We deconstruct your business into atomic function modules with strict SLAs. For each module, we determine whether it's executed by a human, an AI agent, or a hybrid. This enables iterative optimization across the entire company.

03 — Processes

Deconstructing how work actually gets done. Workflows mapped, bottlenecks identified, dependencies revealed.

04 — AI Opportunities

Identifying where AI can genuinely transform a process, function, or role — not just automate for the sake of it.

05 — Priorities

Scoring each initiative by impact, complexity, cost, and risk. Choosing what to do first — and what to skip.

06 — Roadmap

Building a sequential plan of changes. Quick wins first, strategic projects next. Clear timelines and milestones.

07 — Implementation

Designing and deploying solutions together with the technical team. From architecture to production.
