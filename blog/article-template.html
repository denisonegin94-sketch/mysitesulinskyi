How AI reduces property valuation time by 80%

Oct 12 • 5 min read

Most real estate businesses still rely on manual data processing. Brokers spend countless hours matching listings, analyzing historical data, and putting together valuation reports. This is a linear process where output is strictly bottlenecked by human hours available.

AI for business is not a matter of individual tools. It is a matter of how the company's work itself should be structured.

The Solution Architecture

By implementing autonomous agents that constantly scrape data sources and utilize LLMs to structure unstructured data, we decoupled growth from headcount. Agents can operate 24/7, assessing market anomalies immediately and surfacing them to brokers only when high-value human judgment is needed.
